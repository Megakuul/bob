   and rewrites bob's own cache `.bob/eval/{key,index.json,gocache/…}`; no working file outside

   `.bob/` as bob's ignored state, and R-FW-043 requires the evaluator cache there), or (b) bob
   check evaluates without persisting .bob/eval (pkg/engine/eval owner; costs an evaluator compile
   on every check while go.mod is edited). Needs a ruling; nothing applied.
