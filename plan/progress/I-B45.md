### B-076 (import 10,000 source dirs) — limit is nix-store --import
Phases (instrumented private copy, TestImportManyTiming): serialise+hash 0.1-0.55 s, validity
0.3-0.4 s, `nix-store --import` of 10,000 paths 7.5-8.5 s (single process, ~0.8 ms/path; vmstat:
one core busy, 3-28 % iowait, no fsync calls in strace). Splitting the stream over 4 or 8
concurrent `nix-store --import` processes did not help (8.8 / 7.9 s): concurrent writers
serialise on the SQLite write lock (Nix's busy handler sleeps). The budget row is "status
proposal, busy: true, source: 3-10 s with nix-store --add (loaded)". Options for the owner: keep
5 s and accept it fails on a loaded host, raise the limit, or a design where bob unpacks the
trees itself and registers all paths in one batch (`nix-store --register-validity`) — a change to
how bob writes the store (needs an ADR; Nix owns the store database).

### R-GO-002b / R-GO-003a — cgo in the developer environment (ruling needed)

exclude all Go files` (ffi has only cgo files; `vet: ffi_test.go: undefined: NumHex`). bob env /

small fix.
