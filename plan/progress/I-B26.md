## DECISIONS
- A160 The evaluation result of an evalbuild job is taken into the build when the job has
  succeeded and waits for promotion (not only at its final pass): pipelines then show running
  during promotion (§6.9.4 "pending (queued), running, success"). The report is the same
  verified one (signed EvalSHA, Verify against the selection); applying it twice is idempotent
  (pipelines by name, unique failed labels and errors, nodes per job).
- A161 Builds.Create writes its three intent-first index entries concurrently (safe.Go): they
  are independent documents and the order among them carries no meaning (the record is still
  created after all three); this keeps webhook → BuildRecord within B-071 on the devstack.
- A162 Supersede is decided by the newer build only when its pipelines are known (API, retry,
  rerun, remote builds at plan time; forge event builds after an evaluation that matched at
  least one pipeline). Before, every push to a non-default branch superseded the running build
  of that branch at plan time, even pushes that match no pipeline and end SKIPPED (R-PIPE-005
  (1), §05.6.2 "newer build ... with concurrency=supersede": the concurrency setting belongs to
  pipelines). Queue waiting is unchanged (decided at plan time).
